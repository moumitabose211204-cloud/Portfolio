About me

Curious by nature.
Analytical by practice.

I enjoy finding the signal inside noisy data. My work combines analytical thinking, thoughtful visualization and AI-assisted workflows to turn raw information into insights people can understand and act on.

I’m especially interested in projects where technology meets community impact—building practical solutions, explaining findings clearly and learning alongside people who care about responsible innovation.

My approach

Rigorous analysis, clearly communicated.

Frame

Clarify the problem, audience, success criteria and decisions the work should support.

Prepare

Inspect, clean and validate the data while documenting assumptions and limitations.

Analyze

Explore patterns, test ideas and select methods that match the question—not the trend.

Communicate

Turn findings into focused visuals, responsible conclusions and practical next steps.
